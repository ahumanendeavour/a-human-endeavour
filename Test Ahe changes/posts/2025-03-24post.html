24/03/2025

    Auditions - 3
  • 📹Self-Tapes - 2
  • 🤝In-person - 1

    Of those auditions...
  • 🎭Theatre - 1
  • 🏷️Adverts - 2

Admittedly not a great quantity of auditions to kick off the year, but I really couldn't argue with the quality.

Both adverts would have offered a fair bit of travel and a lovey working experience with great directors and fun scripts to shoot. The money would have been a lovely way to kick the year off as well.

The theatre audition was a very happy surprise. Without saying too much, it would be an understudy role for a piece of new writing, led by an incredibly talented, big-name director, and would be my London stage debut. I was very very happy with the tape. Admittedly as I write this I haven't heard back about it either way, though I'm not getting my hopes up. That way, madness lies.
