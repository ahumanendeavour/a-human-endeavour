[about]

Actor, 30s, he/him, Scottish

I've been working in the creative arts for about 12 years now, and in that time I've done a bit of everything. I've acted on stage and TV, toured internationally on big theatre projects as well as my own solo work, and even done a couple of audiobooks. I've been a puppeteer, an acting teacher, a writer, and occasionally a simulated patient for trainee doctors.

I recently quit my job and am finally making a go at doing this full time, and I think I'm just about getting away with it...

Every month I post a breakdown of all my auditions, as well as updates from my personal life. I want to provide a little peek behind the curtain into the life of a working actor to show what goes into making a career for yourself in the industry.

Since I want to maintain some level of anonymity I won't necessarily spill the beans on specific projects, but I'll try and be as much of an open book as I can.

On the home page you can find a bit about what I'm up to right now, an archive of all my blog posts, as well as my contact details to get in touch.
